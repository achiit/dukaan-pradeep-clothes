About Us

A family shop on Devnathpur Road.

I am Pradeep Vishwakarma. I grew up around Bhadohi, where Banarasi weaving is part of everyday life. Watching my relatives pick sarees for weddings taught me how much a good fabric matters.

In 2026 I opened Pradeep Collection in Vedpur so our neighbours do not have to travel far for good clothes. My promise is simple: show honest quality, quote a fair price, and never rush you.

Come in, touch the fabric, and compare. If you cannot visit, just send a message and I will reply myself.
